About

A small studio with a narrow focus

We started in 2019 after years of agency work where projects were sold by people who never touched the code. Northwind is the opposite: the people you meet on the first call are the people who design and build the thing.

That means we take on fewer projects, keep timelines honest and write documentation you can hand to the next team. Most of our clients come from referrals, which is the only marketing we have ever needed.

  • Founded 2019, Rotterdam
  • Team 5 people, no subcontractors
  • Focus B2B tools and public-sector services
  • Typical run 6 to 14 weeks per project
